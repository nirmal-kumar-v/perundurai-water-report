About Us

Understanding the Water Crisis & Our Mission

Mission

To create a transparent, accountable, and data-driven water problem reporting system that empowers citizens, enforces administrative responsibility, and drives sustainable solutions for water quality issues in Perundurai.

Vision

A Perundurai where every citizen's water problem is heard, tracked, and resolved within 72 hours; where data guides policy decisions; and where transparency creates the pressure needed for permanent infrastructure improvements.

The SIPCOT Water Crisis

TWAD Study, August 2025: Groundwater in 25+ villages around Perundurai has been contaminated for 25 years by SIPCOT Industrial Complex.

Critical Findings:

  • TDS Levels: 2000+ mg/L (4x the safe limit of 500-1500 mg/L)
  • Heavy Metals: Iron, fluoride, sulphate, chloride above safe drinking water standards
  • Sources: 47 textile industries, 18 tanneries, 12 chemical factories in SIPCOT dumping untreated sewage
  • Affected Areas: Elithingal Patti, Kuttam Palayam, Kasilingam Palayam, Kadappamadi, Velliyam Palayam, Ingur, and 19+ other villages
  • Health Impact: Increased respiratory diseases, water-related illnesses, vulnerable populations severely affected
  • Current Status: Common Effluent Treatment Plant (CETP) promised 10+ years ago, still not built

Why This Website Was Created:

The traditional complaint system fails because:

  • No tracking - Complaints disappear into bureaucracy
  • No accountability - Authorities can delay indefinitely
  • No data - Officials don't know where problems are most severe
  • No transparency - Public doesn't know if work is being done
  • No proof - No documentation that repairs actually happened

How Our System Solves These Problems

Citizen Empowerment

Citizens become researchers and advocates. Aadhaar-verified accounts prevent fraud. Endorsement system shows one problem isn't just one person's issue - it affects 50+ neighbors.

Enforced Accountability

72-hour deadline with auto-escalation to Supreme Authority. Admin delays have consequences. Public visibility creates pressure for action.

Data-Driven Decisions

Supreme dashboard shows: Ward 1 = 40% of all problems (need more resources there). Problem type analysis identifies SIPCOT contamination as 35% of issues (need CETP urgently).

Transparent Proof

Photo evidence required at each stage. Before/After photos prove work was actually done. Timeline shows exactly when each step happened.
