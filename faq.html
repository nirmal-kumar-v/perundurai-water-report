Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Using Our Platform

Registration & Login

Step-by-step registration:
  1. Click "Register" on the homepage
  2. Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number
  3. Fill in name, phone (10 digits), email, and address
  4. Select your ward from the dropdown
  5. Upload a clear photo (JPG/PNG, max 5MB) for KYC verification
  6. Set a password (minimum 6 characters)
  7. Accept terms and click "Register Now"

Your account will be created instantly. No OTP required - registration is immediate.

Aadhaar verification serves three purposes:
  • Authenticity: Prevents fake accounts and false complaints
  • Accountability: Links you to your complaints so you're responsible
  • Government Recognition: Makes the portal officially credible with authorities
Your Aadhaar is stored securely and never shared without permission.

You can login using either:
  • Your registered email address
  • Your 12-digit Aadhaar number
Plus your password. Choose whichever is easier for you.

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. We'll display your registered phone number so you can verify your identity. Once verified, you can set a new password.

Reporting Problems

To report a problem:
  1. Login to your dashboard
  2. Click "Report New Problem" tab
  3. Select the problem category (12 options)
  4. Select your ward/location
  5. Describe the problem (max 500 characters)
  6. Upload at least one photo showing the problem
  7. Set severity level (auto-calculated)
  8. Click "Submit Report"
You'll get a unique Problem ID (PROB-2025-XXXX) for tracking.

Timeline of your problem:
  1. Immediately: Problem appears on admin dashboard and public tracker
  2. Within 24 hours: Admin marks "Noted" (acknowledges)
  3. Within 2 days: Admin marks "Pending" (assigns team)
  4. Within 3 days: Team starts work, marked "Working"
  5. Within 4-5 days: Problem resolved, marked "Completed"
If 72 hours pass without action: Problem automatically escalates to Supreme Authority.

Yes, but only while the problem is in "Reported" status (before admin acknowledges).
  • Once admin marks "Noted", you can view but not edit
  • Deletion is permanent once admin starts working on it
This prevents losing important problem data once action has begun.

For emergencies (no water at all, chemical contamination, etc.):
  1. Report on platform immediately
  2. Set severity as "Critical"
  3. Call TWAD directly: 0424-2260621
  4. Mention your problem ID
Calling ensures immediate attention while the portal tracks everything.

Tracking & Updates

Three ways to track:
  1. Go to your dashboard → "My Reports" tab
  2. Click the problem ID to view full timeline
  3. You'll see every status change with timestamp and photos
Similar to tracking an Amazon order - complete transparency at each step.

You'll get notifications at every step:
  • In-app: Notification badge in your dashboard
  • Email: Update sent to your registered email
  • SMS: Text message to your phone
All notifications are logged in your "Notification Center" even after 3 days.

Escalation happens when:
  • Admin hasn't updated the problem after 72 hours (3 days)
  • Problem automatically moves to Supreme Authority dashboard
  • Supreme Authority then investigates or forces the admin to act
This creates accountability - no admin can ignore problems forever.

Endorsements & Community

Endorsement is like "upvoting" or "liking" a problem:
  • If you have the same problem, click the heart icon to endorse
  • Each endorsement shows: "50 people have this problem"
  • Problems with more endorsements get higher priority
  • Prevents single complaints from being ignored
Why it matters: Shows collective impact. A problem affecting 50 people is more urgent than one affecting 1 person.

  • Leave comments saying you have the same problem
  • Share additional details or photos
  • Discuss solutions with other residents
  • Admin will see collective feedback and prioritize accordingly
Inappropriate comments are moderated and removed.

For Administrators & Authority

Admin workflow:
  1. View all problems on interactive map
  2. Click problem → Change status: Reported → Noted → Pending → Working → Completed
  3. Assign to field teams (Team A, B, C, D)
  4. Upload evidence photos as work progresses
  5. Each update triggers user notification
Admins are accountable - 72-hour deadline enforced by auto-escalation.

Supreme Authority sees:
  • Analytics: Overall resolution rate, average time, trends
  • Ward analysis: Where problems are most severe
  • Admin performance: Who resolves fastest, who delays
  • Escalated problems: All unresolved problems after 72 hours
  • Reports: Exportable data for policy decisions
This creates pressure for everyone to perform better.
